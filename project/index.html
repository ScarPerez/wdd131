THE F1 DICTIONARY

Speak F1.

Start with some of the most common words and phrases you'll hear during a Formula 1 weekend.

Drag Reduction System

A system that allows a driver to reduce aerodynamic drag and increase speed on certain parts of the circuit.

Pole Position

The first starting position on the grid, earned by the fastest driver during qualifying.

Pit Stop

A stop in the pit lane where the team can change tires, make adjustments, or repair the car.

Undercut

A strategy where a driver pits before a rival in an attempt to gain track position after the rival stops.

Safety Car

A vehicle that enters the track during dangerous conditions, slowing the field and keeping the cars together.

Fastest Lap

The quickest lap recorded by a driver during the race.

THE RACE WEEKEND

Three stages. One race.

An F1 weekend is more than just Sunday. Learn what happens from practice to qualifying and finally race day.

PRACTICE

Learn the track.

Drivers use practice sessions to learn the circuit, test setups, and prepare their cars for qualifying and the race.

QUALIFYING

Fight for position.

Qualifying determines where each driver will start the race. The fastest driver earns pole position.

RACE DAY

Lights out.

The race begins when the lights go out. Drivers compete for position, points, and ultimately the podium.
