Site Name

Formula 1 Rookie Guide (RF1)

Formula 1 Rookie Guide (RF1) was chosen because it clearly represents the purpose of the website: helping beginners understand and enjoy Formula 1. The word Rookie emphasizes that the site is designed for new fans who want to learn the sport in a simple, visual, and engaging way.

The abbreviation RF1 serves as the website's visual identity. The logo features a modern and minimal design inspired by racing circuits, reinforcing the educational and premium feel of the project.

Optional Domain: formula1rookieguide.com

Site Purpose

Formula 1 Rookie Guide is an educational website created for people who are new to Formula 1. Instead of focusing on race results or championship standings, the website explains the fundamentals of the sport through clear content, immersive visuals, and interactive experiences.

Visitors will be able to explore current Formula 1 drivers and teams, understand common racing terminology, learn how a race weekend works, and reinforce their knowledge through an interactive quiz built with JavaScript.

Scenarios

  • What do common Formula 1 terms such as DRS, DNF, Pole Position, and Safety Car mean?
  • How does a Formula 1 race weekend work from practice sessions to the Grand Prix?
  • Who are the current Formula 1 drivers and which teams do they represent?
  • How can I quickly find information about my favorite Formula 1 driver or team?
  • How can I test my Formula 1 knowledge after learning the basics?

Design Concept

RF1 is designed as an immersive learning experience for people who are new to Formula 1. Inspired by the clean aesthetics of the McLaren and Aston Martin websites, the design combines full-width images, cinematic videos, and minimalist layouts to guide users naturally through the content.

Instead of presenting large amounts of information at once, each section focuses on a single topic and encourages users to continue exploring. The goal is to make learning Formula 1 simple, engaging, and visually memorable.

Website Structure

  • Home – Welcome page with hero video and introduction.
  • Drivers – Current Formula 1 drivers with search functionality.
  • Teams – Information about the current Formula 1 teams.
  • Race Weekend – Explanation of how a Formula 1 weekend works.
  • Quiz – Interactive quiz to test the user's knowledge.
